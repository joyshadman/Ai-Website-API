Designed for depth

Every feature serves a single purpose: helping you settle into presence.

Flexible Timer

Set any duration with interval chimes. Choose from bowl, bell, or silence.

Breath Guidance

Optional visual and haptic pacing for box breathing, 4-7-8, or custom rhythms.

Shared Sessions

Meditate with a friend or group in real time, no accounts required.

Gentle Insights

See patterns over weeks, not days. No streaks, no pressure—just perspective.
