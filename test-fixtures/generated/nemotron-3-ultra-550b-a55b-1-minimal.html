About Me

I'm Mara, a senior UX consultant based in Copenhagen. Over the past decade I've worked with early-stage startups, scale-ups, and enterprise teams across Europe and the US.

My approach blends rigorous research with pragmatic design—I believe the best solutions come from deeply understanding the problem before jumping to pixels. I've led design for products in fintech, healthcare, SaaS, and consumer tech, and I'm equally comfortable running discovery workshops as I am shipping production-ready component libraries.

Before going independent, I was Design Lead at a Series B SaaS company where I built the design function from the ground up. I also teach a part-time course on interaction design at a local design school.
