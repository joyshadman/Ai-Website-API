About Ines

Driven by a passion for wandering and a love for light, Ines Marchetti travels the globe seeking stories to tell through her lens. With over a decade of experience, she has photographed in more than 30 countries, turning fleeting moments into lasting memories.

Her work has been featured in National Geographic, Lonely Planet, and many other publications. When not shooting, she teaches photography workshops, sharing her technique and eye for detail.
